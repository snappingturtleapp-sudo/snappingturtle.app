Frequently Asked Questions

Real questions about sending, pricing, delivery, and privacy.

How can I print the digital images on my phone and send them to someone by mail?

Just visit snappingturtle.app — or Download from Apple Store or Get it on Google Play. Simply choose your photos, frame the visible print area with our awesome framing tools, write a personal message, enter the recipient's name and address, review and check out. A professionally printed 4x6 photo package, including a rigid cardboard insert to protect the photos, and a temporary paper photo sleeve, is mailed directly to them with a tracking number.

How much does it cost to send a printed photo package?

We have three packages ranging from $25 for 5 photos, $35 for 10 photos, or $50 for 20 photos, plus applicable sales tax. Every package includes a personal message for the recipient, created by you, and printed on the same paper as your 4x6 photographs.

How long does delivery take?

Production and delivery typically take 6 to 10 business days. Once your package ships, you can track its exact delivery status anytime at snappingturtle.app/track.html. If you download the app, you will automatically get tracking details updated in your “My Orders” page once the package has been printed and shipped.

What if my photos arrive damaged?

We ask that you document the damage photographically and send us a photo of the damaged packaging and photographs, along with a photograph of the packing slip order details. You must report damage claims within 48 hours of your package's actual delivery time, as confirmed by the carrier's tracking details. You can submit damaged goods to us at snappingturtle.app/damage-claim.html.

Can I write a personal message to go with the photos?

Yes — every order includes a personal message that you can say what you want to the recipient of the gift. The words you type will be printed on a 4x6 card printed on the exact same photo paper that your photographs are printed on.

Is my and my recipient's information kept private?

Yes. Recipient names and addresses are used only to build the actual shipping request — they are never stored in our own database. Every major photo platform treats your and your recipient’s Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and images as data to be mined, creating security liabilities and hacking risks. SnappingTurtle App treats PII as a toxic asset. Because PII never touches our backend, that in itself is an actual superpower, completely eliminating the risk of data breaches.

How is this different from just texting someone a photo?

In an era defined by digital chaos, our connections have become both instant and fleeting. While technology has successfully bridged geographic distances, it has hollowed out the emotional weight of our physical interactions. The difference is, this app is for the moments in life when you truly care about someone and want to send the perfect "I was thinking about you" gift in a way a notification, text message or social media post never can.

Does SnappingTurtle store or track my photos after I send them?

Order details, including your photos, are kept for 30 days solely so a delivery damage claim can be processed if something arrives damaged; after that window, they're not retained.

Do my photos arrive in a discreet envelope?

Yes. Every package ships in a plain, unbranded mailer with a generic “Sender: Photo Print Lab” label, so there's a hint at what's inside, photographs, that lets the mail carrier know not to bend the envelope. In addition, every order includes a real carrier tracking number so you can monitor the delivery status in real time.

Is SnappingTurtle App a subscription based service, or is it free to use?

We built this app to be completely free to use. Most platforms suffer from feature creep, forcing account creation, log in screens, verification codes, two-factor authentication, complex layouts, and subscriptions. SnappingTurtle App has completely eliminated the friction. SnappingTurtle App requires no user account, no login, and no password. You open the app, make your selections, and send. By keeping it free, you can go from an emotional thought to a finalized shipment whenever you feel like it.

How long will a printed photo last compared to digital?

Digital photos suffer from immediate decay, buried under thousands of screenshots and memes. Even if they survive on a cloud drive, formats change and platforms lapse. We print on archival, premium photo paper engineered to last at least 50 years without fading. Three decades from now, when today's smartphones are obsolete, the physical photograph that you sent will still be a treasured heirloom holding its vibrant color, tactile weight, and emotional value.

Why doesn't SnappingTurtle require an account?

When we built this business, we intentionally designed our privacy architecture as our superpower. SnappingTurtle App treats PII as a toxic asset. This is our biggest differentiator. Other photo printing services are notorious for forcing users to create accounts, save "projects," and agree to marketing emails. Our completely frictionless, anonymous pipeline is incredibly rare in the consumer photo space. SnappingTurtle deliberately has none of that — you select your photos, write a message, and check out as a guest, the same way every time.

Does SnappingTurtle have the cheapest price for printing digital images on photo paper?

No. This app is considerably more expensive than other platforms which print your digital images. If you want to print your personal vacation photos at a cheap price, there are far better options out there. There is no other mainstream company providing the exact combination of features as we do. We are the world’s most frictionless way of sending the gift of real 4x6 photos and a message card.

Why does SnappingTurtle print everyday, candid photos instead of polished professional shots?

These aren't meant to be gallery-quality prints behind glass — they're "fridge" photos, the kind that go on the most-seen wall in someone's home: the refrigerator door. Sending an unpolished, real moment says you want to be part of someone's daily life, not just their highlight reel.

What happens to photos that never get printed?

Digital photos tend to get buried — lost in camera-roll clutter, or stranded when a format or platform eventually changes. A physical print doesn't depend on any of that; it just sits where you put it, unaffected by whatever happens to your phone or cloud account.
