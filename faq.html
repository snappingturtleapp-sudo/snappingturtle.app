Frequently Asked Questions

Real questions about sending, pricing, delivery, and privacy.

How can I send a real printed photo to someone by mail?

Just visit snappingturtle.app — no app to download. Choose your photos, write a message, enter the recipient's name and address, and check out. A professionally printed 4x6 photo package is mailed directly to them.

Do I need to create an account to send photos by mail?

No. SnappingTurtle is built with no account, no sign-up, and no password required — you can send a package from your very first visit.

How much does it cost to send a printed photo package?

Starting at $25 for 5 photos, $35 for 10 photos, or $50 for 20 photos, plus applicable sales tax. Every package includes both a personal message printed on the packing slip and on 4x6 photo paper.

Can I send more than one photo in the same mailing?

Yes — five photos are included in a single $25 order.

How long does delivery take?

Production and delivery typically take 6 to 10 business days. Once your package ships, you can track its exact delivery status anytime at snappingturtle.app/track.html.

What if my photos arrive damaged?

You can report it within 48 hours of your package's actual delivery time, as confirmed by the carrier's tracking details.

Can I write a personal message to go with the photos?

Yes — every order includes your personal message printed on the packing slip, plus a dedicated keepsake photo carrying that same message on premium photo paper.

Is my recipient's information kept private?

Yes. Recipient names and addresses are used only to build the actual shipping request — they are never stored in our own database.

How is this different from just texting someone a photo?

A text disappears into a feed. A printed photograph is a physical object — one that can sit on a refrigerator, in a frame, or in a drawer for years, long after a digital message would have been forgotten.
