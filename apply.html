Influencer Commission Workflow

Turn your audience into income

Here's exactly how it works, start to finish — no surprises, no fine print you can't see up front.

Tracking & Your Dashboard

Once approved, you get your own promo code and a private dashboard, unlocked by a secure access token emailed only to you. Every sale that uses your code is tracked automatically. Your "Request Payment" button stays greyed out until you reach 100 cleared sales — the moment you hit it, the button activates.

The 30-Day Safety Window

Every sale sits in a 30-day window before it counts toward your payout — this protects against refunds and chargebacks. After 30 days with no issue, it becomes a genuine, cleared sale.

Requesting Payment & Review

Once you click "Request Payment," we compile your cleared sales into an itemized PDF statement and email it to you with a secure link. You review it and check a certification box confirming the total is correct — that's your digital signature. Once approved, your sales move to "Cleared" and are queued for payment.

Getting Paid — QuickBooks Bill Pay (Standard ACH)

We pay via QuickBooks Bill Pay, free Standard ACH direct deposit — no fees. QuickBooks emails you a secure, one-time link to enter your own bank details directly with them. We never see, collect, or store your routing or account numbers at any time. Standard ACH typically takes 3-5 business days to arrive once payment is scheduled.

Payments are processed on our regular payout cycle (the 1st or 15th of the month), so depending on timing, it can take up to 45 days from a sale's original date to when payment is scheduled.

Commission Rate

Flat $1.00 for every valid, fully paid sale that uses your promo code — no cap, no tiers. 100 cleared sales = a $100 payout request.

Your Dashboard at a Glance

  • Total Referrals (Lifetime) — every successful sale ever attributed to your code
  • Total Earnings (Lifetime) — the total you've actually been paid
  • Next Milestone Progress — how close you are to your next 100-sale payout
  • Pending / Cleared / Locked for Review — exactly where every sale sits in the 30-day and review process
  • Payout History & Documents — every past statement, always available to you

The Fine Print

The full legal terms — including the Independent Contractor Agreement you'll sign once approved — are presented separately before your account is activated. This page is a plain-English summary, not the contract itself.
